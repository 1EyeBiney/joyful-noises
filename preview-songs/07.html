Track 7

Lead Us to the Father

About the song

Jesus repeatedly sought the Father in ordinary life, before decisions, and in Gethsemane. The song asks Him to lead us into the same prayerful trust and reminds us that the Spirit helps when words fail.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

Mark 1:35 Early in the morning, while it was still dark, Jesus got up and went out to a solitary place to pray.

Luke 11:1 One day in a place where Jesus had just finished praying, one of His disciples requested, “Lord, teach us to pray, just as John taught his disciples.”

Luke 11:2 So Jesus told them, “When you pray, say: ‘Father, hallowed be Your name. Your kingdom come.

Luke 11:3 Give us each day our daily bread.

Luke 11:4 And forgive us our sins, for we also forgive everyone who sins against us. And lead us not into temptation.’”

Romans 8:26 In the same way, the Spirit helps us in our weakness. For we do not know how we ought to pray, but the Spirit Himself intercedes for us with groans too deep for words.

Further reading: Luke 5:16; Luke 6:12–13; Luke 22:39–46; John 14:6; Hebrews 4:14–16; Romans 8:15–16, 26–27.

Brian’s reflections

We first tried to remake “Be Still,” but its original purpose depended on the juxtaposition between a restless electronic track and the command to be still. Once that tension was removed, forcing it into a congregational arrangement did not make sense. We chose a new subject that had been important in our church. We had just heard a sermon about when and how Jesus prayed. Becoming more like Christ includes learning from how often He spoke with the Father.

The first title and version, “Teach Us to Pray,” leaned too close melodically to the familiar hymn “Have Thine Own Way, Lord,” and its verses did not follow a satisfying lyrical pattern. We kept working, changed the title, revised the verses, tried different singers and styles, and eventually arrived at “Lead Us to the Father.” This is not a remix of track 7. It is the companion album admitting that a different song belonged in that place.
