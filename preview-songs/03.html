Track 3

You Are Calling

About the song

After recognizing God’s nearness, the listener hears the Savior’s personal call. Jesus calls His sheep by name; the response is to leave old securities, take the next step, and follow Him.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

John 10:27 My sheep listen to My voice; I know them, and they follow Me.

John 10:28 I give them eternal life, and they will never perish. No one can snatch them out of My hand.

Matthew 4:19 “Come, follow Me,” Jesus said, “and I will make you fishers of men.”

Matthew 4:20 And at once they left their nets and followed Him.

Further reading: John 10:3–4; John 1:35–39; Philippians 3:12–14.

Brian’s reflections

We attempted a more obvious acoustic folk-worship version, but I did not prefer the results. In the end, I kept the same recording used on the original album. That choice became part of the philosophy of this edition: a different version was not automatically a better one, and I did not need to replace something simply to make the two track lists look symmetrical. This song already carried the call and response clearly enough.
