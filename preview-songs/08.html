Track 8

Come As You Are

About the song

We do not make ourselves acceptable before approaching Jesus. We come honestly and empty-handed, receiving the rest, mercy, and grace only He can give.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

Matthew 11:28 Come to Me, all you who are weary and burdened, and I will give you rest.

Matthew 11:29 Take My yoke upon you and learn from Me, for I am gentle and humble in heart, and you will find rest for your souls.

Matthew 11:30 For My yoke is easy and My burden is light.”

Romans 5:8 But God proves His love for us in this: While we were still sinners, Christ died for us.

Hebrews 4:15 For we do not have a high priest who is unable to sympathize with our weaknesses, but we have one who was tempted in every way that we are, yet was without sin.

Hebrews 4:16 Let us then approach the throne of grace with confidence, so that we may receive mercy and find grace to help us in our time of need.

Further reading: Luke 15:11–24; Psalm 51:16–17; Ephesians 2:8–9.

Brian’s reflections

The original “Come As You Are” affected me more deeply than any of the other generated results. For this edition, I asked for the simplest possible treatment: piano only. The saved recording later received the informal “wootsie remix” filename, as it reminds me of my brother Bruce. The uncluttered arrangement leaves the invitation exposed. I often feel as though I have to “get my act together” before I can come to Jesus, yet He never turns me away, no matter the state in which I come to Him. There is less production between the listener and that invitation: bring what you are, receive who Christ is, and stop waiting to become worthy before coming to Him.
