About this version

Pastor John of Hope Christian Church encouraged this gentle slide-guitar version of “Before the Morning.” Brian chose it to open the acoustic album. We worship on Sundays at 10 a.m. at Hope Christian Church, 3525 Senour Road, Indianapolis, IN 46239. Come on by—we’d love to see you.
