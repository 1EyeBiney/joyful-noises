Track 6

Signal Through the Noise

About the song

Many voices compete for attention, but the voice of Jesus is different. We learn His voice through His Word and character, trust His truth, and follow His direction.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

John 10:27 My sheep listen to My voice; I know them, and they follow Me.

Isaiah 30:21 And whether you turn to the right or to the left, your ears will hear this command behind you: “This is the way. Walk in it.”

Romans 12:2 Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewing of your mind. Then you will be able to test and approve what is the good, pleasing, and perfect will of God.

Further reading: Psalm 46:10; John 14:6.

Brian’s reflections

This seemed like one of the original songs that might translate more naturally if the lyrics were cleaned up and the arrangement made less busy. The companion version keeps the problem of competing voices but states the response more plainly: seek Christ’s truth, learn His voice through His Word, and follow Him. In a quieter arrangement, the song’s message can stand in front of the production rather than fighting through it.
