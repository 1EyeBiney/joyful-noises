Track 13

A Joyful Noise

About the song

Our joy comes from belonging to the risen Jesus and knowing we will be with Him forever. Present pain is acknowledged, but it does not erase living hope. This is the final song and the message listeners carry out the door.

Brian’s reflections

This was the selected acoustic recording that surprised me most when I first heard it. The original electronic song closes with short phrases, repetition, and the kind of rhythmic release I enjoy. Those lyrics belonged to that production, so the companion version needed a fuller rewrite. The acoustic-pop lyric states the same hope in a form that is easier to follow together: God has made us His, the risen Jesus has broken death’s claim, and one day tears and grief will end. Both albums still leave through the same door. The sound changes, but the final word remains joyful praise rooted in eternity with Christ.
