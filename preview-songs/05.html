Track 5

Still You Stay

About the song

Seasons, plans, and circumstances change, but Jesus remains faithful. The song grounds security in who God is without promising freedom from grief or trouble.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

Hebrews 13:8 Jesus Christ is the same yesterday and today and forever.

Psalm 46:1 For the choirmaster. Of the sons of Korah. According to Alamoth. A song. God is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in times of trouble.

Psalm 46:2 Therefore we will not fear, though the earth is transformed and the mountains are toppled into the depths of the seas,

Psalm 46:3 though their waters roar and foam and the mountains quake in the surge. Selah

Lamentations 3:22 Because of the loving devotion of the LORD we are not consumed, for His mercies never fail.

Lamentations 3:23 They are new every morning; great is Your faithfulness!

Further reading: Psalm 102:25–27; James 1:17; Isaiah 40:8; Malachi 3:6.

Brian’s reflections

I expected this track to need a substantial rewrite for people to sing with it, and it did. The acoustic version uses a more regular structure and simpler movement through change, grief, refuge, and God’s faithfulness. I wanted the words to remain realistic. The song does not say that following Christ prevents the ground from shaking. It says that when circumstances change, His character does not.
