Track 12

Be The Way

About the song

Jesus is the Way and the Light. We ask Him to make His love visible through everyday relationships, kindness, service, and a life that points others toward Christ. This began as promotional music for Hope Christian Church’s weekly Be The Way podcast. Visit the Be The Way Podcast on YouTube.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

John 14:5 “Lord,” said Thomas, “we do not know where You are going, so how can we know the way?”

John 14:6 Jesus answered, “I am the way and the truth and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me.

Matthew 5:14 You are the light of the world. A city on a hill cannot be hidden.

Matthew 5:15 Neither do people light a lamp and put it under a basket. Instead, they set it on a stand, and it gives light to everyone in the house.

Matthew 5:16 In the same way, let your light shine before men, that they may see your good deeds and glorify your Father in heaven.

John 13:34 A new commandment I give you: Love one another. As I have loved you, so you also must love one another.

John 13:35 By this everyone will know that you are My disciples, if you love one another.”

Further reading: John 8:12; Acts 9:2; Acts 19:9, 23; Ephesians 2:8–10; Matthew 28:19–20; Galatians 2:20; Lamentations 3:22–23.
