Track 4

In Your Arms

About the song

A weary believer brings doubts and tears to Christ and rests securely in His faithful care. The acoustic rewrite became a substantially different song and received its own title.

Biblical foundation

Romans 8:35 Who shall separate us from the love of Christ? Shall trouble or distress or persecution or famine or nakedness or danger or sword?

Romans 8:38 For I am convinced that neither death nor life, neither angels nor principalities, neither the present nor the future, nor any powers,

Romans 8:39 neither height nor depth, nor anything else in all creation, will be able to separate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord.

Further reading: Psalm 139:7–10; Deuteronomy 31:8; Hebrews 13:5–8.

Brian’s reflections

This began as an attempt to reduce the energy of “Nothing Between.” The first new versions still felt too intense, partly because the original lyrics naturally pushed the music in that direction. When we rewrote them, the result became gentler and more personal: bringing doubts, tears, and the weight of yesterday into the security of Christ’s care. I liked the new result, but it no longer felt honest to call it “Nothing Between.” Naming it “In Your Arms” acknowledged that a new song had emerged. It was also around this fourth track that I realized we were no longer making a few alternate versions. A companion album was taking shape.
