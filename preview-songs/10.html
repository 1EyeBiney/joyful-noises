Track 10

Risen Son

About the song

Jesus has risen, and His victory gives believers living hope. The song moves from the empty tomb to His recognizable wounds and looks toward the believer’s future resurrection.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

Matthew 28:5 But the angel said to the women, “Do not be afraid, for I know that you are looking for Jesus, who was crucified.

Matthew 28:6 He is not here; He has risen, just as He said! Come, see the place where He lay.

Romans 6:9 For we know that since Christ was raised from the dead, He cannot die again; death no longer has dominion over Him.

John 20:27 Then Jesus said to Thomas, “Put your finger here and look at My hands. Reach out your hand and put it into My side. Stop doubting and believe.”

John 20:28 Thomas replied, “My Lord and my God!”

John 20:29 Jesus said to him, “Because you have seen Me, you have believed; blessed are those who have not seen and yet have believed.”

Further reading: Luke 24:1–6; Romans 4:25.

Brian’s reflections

This was the most difficult song to adapt. We tried a congregational worship approach, a warm acoustic-pop version with a male singer, and other directions. Some sounded too much like a live audience recording. Others felt forced or unlikely to remain memorable. We finally returned to a female voice and allowed the arrangement to move between exposed acoustic sections and fuller, highly produced passages with layered vocals. The goal was a radio-ready song with intimacy and lift, without the auditorium sound that had distracted from earlier attempts.
