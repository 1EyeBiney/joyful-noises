Track 2

Breathe Again

About the song

God is near to the weary and brokenhearted. The One who first gave life remains present through His Spirit and can breathe new life into what feels dry, exhausted, or silent.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

Acts 17:27 God intended that they would seek Him and perhaps reach out for Him and find Him, though He is not far from each one of us.

Acts 17:28 ‘For in Him we live and move and have our being.’ As some of your own poets have said, ‘We are His offspring.’

Psalm 34:18 The LORD is near to the brokenhearted; He saves the contrite in spirit.

Romans 8:11 And if the Spirit of Him who raised Jesus from the dead is living in you, He who raised Christ Jesus from the dead will also give life to your mortal bodies through His Spirit, who lives in you.

Further reading: Psalm 139:7–10; Genesis 2:7; Ezekiel 37:5–10; John 14:16–17; Matthew 28:20.

Brian’s reflections

After looking toward the morning, this song turned inward. Weariness can make God feel distant even when He is not. “Breathe Again” grew from the realization that the breath in my lungs was already evidence of His nearness. I was not asking a distant God to notice me. I was asking the God who was already present to renew hope and faith in the places where they had grown quiet.
