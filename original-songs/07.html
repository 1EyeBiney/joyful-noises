Track 7

Be Still

About the song

The world may shake, roar, fight, and change. God does not compete with the chaos; He calls His people to stop striving and know who He is.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

Psalm 46:1 For the choirmaster. Of the sons of Korah. According to Alamoth. A song. God is our refuge and strength, an ever-present help in times of trouble.

Psalm 46:2 Therefore we will not fear, though the earth is transformed and the mountains are toppled into the depths of the seas,

Psalm 46:3 though their waters roar and foam and the mountains quake in the surge. Selah

Psalm 46:10 “Be still and know that I am God; I will be exalted among the nations, I will be exalted over the earth.”

Further reading: Psalm 46.

Brian’s reflections

The style and the message were deliberately placed in tension: restless breakbeats surrounding the command to be still. Psalm 46 does not describe a calm world. The earth moves, waters roar, and nations rage, yet God remains present. This song reminded me that being still is not denying the chaos. It is stopping long enough to remember who God is inside it. Around the time we made this song, I first realized that the collection of experiments might actually be an album.
