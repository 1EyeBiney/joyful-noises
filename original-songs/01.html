Track 1

Before the Morning

About the song

God does not become faithful when morning arrives. Morning reveals the faithfulness that was already present through the darkness. Praise can begin before circumstances improve, as Paul and Silas sang while still imprisoned.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

Psalm 30:5 For His anger is fleeting, but His favor lasts a lifetime. Weeping may stay the night, but joy comes in the morning.

Habakkuk 3:17 Though the fig tree does not bud and no fruit is on the vines, though the olive crop fails and the fields produce no food, though the sheep are cut off from the fold and no cattle are in the stalls,

Habakkuk 3:18 yet I will exult in the LORD; I will rejoice in the God of my salvation!

Acts 16:25 About midnight Paul and Silas were praying and singing hymns to God, and the other prisoners were listening to them.

Acts 16:26 Suddenly a strong earthquake shook the foundations of the prison. At once all the doors flew open and everyone’s chains came loose.

Further reading: Psalm 130:5–6; Lamentations 3:22–23; Isaiah 50:10.

Brian’s reflections

This was the first new song after “Be The Way,” and it is where the album truly began. I had received news that made the path ahead feel uncertain. I could see the next step, but not much beyond it. I remembered Paul and Silas singing while they were still in prison, before the earthquake and before the doors opened. I knew I needed a different way to handle moments like this. The thought I needed was that morning does not make God faithful. Morning only lets us see the faithfulness that was already carrying us through the night. “I will sing before I see it” became both the center of the song and something I was trying to practice. I spent more time rewriting the Paul-and-Silas passage than any other part of the album because I wanted their extraordinary response to remain connected to ordinary faith: “You were moving in the silence / You were working while I prayed / The morning did not make You faithful / It only showed the way.”
