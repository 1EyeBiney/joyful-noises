Track 11

Alive in You

About the song

Christian life is more than becoming a stronger version of oneself. Christ lives in the believer and becomes the source of how we live, love, and bear fruit in ordinary acts of service.

Biblical foundation

Key passages (Berean Standard Bible)

Galatians 2:20 I have been crucified with Christ, and I no longer live, but Christ lives in me. The life I live in the body, I live by faith in the Son of God, who loved me and gave Himself up for me.

John 15:4 Remain in Me, and I will remain in you. Just as no branch can bear fruit by itself unless it remains in the vine, neither can you bear fruit unless you remain in Me.

John 15:5 I am the vine and you are the branches. The one who remains in Me, and I in him, will bear much fruit. For apart from Me you can do nothing.

2 Corinthians 5:17 Therefore if anyone is in Christ, he is a new creation. The old has passed away. Behold, the new has come!

Further reading: Romans 6:4–11; Colossians 3:3–4; Acts 17:28.

Brian’s reflections

This song was created early in the after-midnight stretch. Our study of Acts repeatedly shows ordinary people living through the presence and power of Christ rather than through their own strength. That matters to me because a life’s value cannot finally rest on ability, employment, accomplishment, or a plan working as expected. Christian life is not merely a stronger version of myself. My life is found in Christ. If a familiar way of serving becomes harder, He remains the source of identity, love, and whatever faithful service comes next.
