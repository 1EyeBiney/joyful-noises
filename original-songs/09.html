Track 9

The Same Hands

About the song

The hands powerful enough to make creation became gentle enough to touch the rejected, humble enough to serve, willing enough to be pierced, and strong enough to hold Christ’s people securely.

Biblical foundation

Brian’s reflections

This song began with a strong image: the hands that shaped creation, touched people others avoided, washed His disciples’ feet, received the nails, and still bore scars after the resurrection are the hands that hold His people now. Our church’s extended attention to the life of Jesus made those actions feel connected rather than isolated: the same Savior moved through all of them. I needed both sides of that image—the power and the gentleness. The One holding me is strong enough to keep me and compassionate enough to meet me where I am.
