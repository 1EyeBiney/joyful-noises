Track 13

A Joyful Noise

About the song

Our joy comes from belonging to the risen Jesus and knowing we will be with Him forever. Present pain is acknowledged, but it does not erase living hope. This is the final song and the message listeners carry out the door.

Biblical foundation

Psalm 100:1 A Psalm of thanksgiving. Make a joyful noise to the LORD, all the earth.

Psalm 100:2 Serve the LORD with gladness; come into His presence with joyful songs.

Psalm 100:3 Know that the LORD is God. It is He who made us, and we are His; we are His people, and the sheep of His pasture.

Psalm 100:4 Enter His gates with thanksgiving and His courts with praise; give thanks to Him and bless His name.

1 Peter 1:4 and into an inheritance that is imperishable, undefiled, and unfading, reserved in heaven for you,

Revelation 21:3 And I heard a loud voice from the throne saying: “Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man, and He will dwell with them. They will be His people, and God Himself will be with them as their God.

Revelation 21:4 ‘He will wipe away every tear from their eyes,’ and there will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the former things have passed away.”

Further reading: Psalm 98:4–6; John 10:27–28; 1 Thessalonians 4:17–18.

Brian’s reflections

This song came after the first numbered collection and gave the albums their name. I do not think I sing well, and I am often hesitant to let other people hear me sing loudly. This project became a way to make a joyful noise through voices that sound more like the joy and beauty I hear in my mind. But the title means more than generated singing. In Acts, Paul and Silas praised God while they were still confined and before they knew what would happen next. Their example helped frame the entire project. A joyful noise is the choice to praise while the outcome is still unknown. I wanted the last song to send people out with living hope: through tears and through the night, we belong to the risen Jesus, and one day we will be with Him forever.
