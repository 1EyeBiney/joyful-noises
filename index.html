Praise before the path is clear

Sometimes the joyful noise begins while the door is still closed and the next step is all you can see.

These songs grew from a difficult season and from lessons our church kept returning to: seek the Word, praise when you are unsure, and talk with the Father. Paul and Silas sang while they were still in prison. Jesus taught His followers to pray. God remains near before the answer arrives.

One collection, two ways to listen

Most people may not share Brian’s favorite electronic styles, so nearly every song also has a warmer version made for a wider audience. The same themes travel through both albums.
